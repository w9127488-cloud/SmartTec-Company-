Food security

Field crops and staples

The staple crops that feed households and economies. We support farmers from planning through post-harvest handling, with attention to soil health, crop health and storage losses.

What we focus on

  • Soil testing, fertility and crop planning
  • Seed and variety selection for local conditions
  • Pest, disease and weed management awareness
  • Harvest, drying, storage and post-harvest handling

Horticulture and floriculture

High-value crops that reward careful management. Quality, timing and compliance decide whether produce reaches premium local and export markets.

What we focus on

  • Efficient irrigation and fertigation
  • Greenhouse and protected cultivation
  • Scouting and integrated pest management
  • Grading, packing and cold-chain planning

Healthy herds

Cattle and dairy

Productive herds begin with good breeding, feeding and housing. We promote record keeping and early recognition of the signs that need veterinary attention.

What we focus on

  • Breeding decisions and herd records
  • Feed planning, fodder and water
  • Housing, comfort and animal welfare
  • Milk hygiene and quality

Examples

Dairy cattleBeef cattleFodder cropsMilk handling
Smallholder resilience

Sheep, goats and pigs

Small ruminants and pigs give households flexible income and a buffer in difficult seasons. Good management keeps animals healthy and enterprises profitable.

What we focus on

  • Breeding records and flock management
  • Nutrition and rangeland use
  • Housing and biosecurity
  • Responsible, welfare-based production

Examples

SheepGoatsPigsRangeland management
Poultry productivity

Poultry and farmed birds

Fast-turnover enterprises that suit both small and larger farms. Flock health, clean housing and feed planning decide the margin.

What we focus on

  • Flock management for layers and broilers
  • Indigenous chicken improvement
  • Clean housing and disease prevention
  • Feed planning and egg or meat marketing

LayersBroilersIndigenous chickensDucksTurkeysGeese
Diversified income

Other farm enterprises

Diversification spreads risk. We help farmers choose enterprises that fit their land, water, skills and a realistic market, rather than following trends.

What we focus on

  • Rabbit keeping for meat and breeding stock
  • Beekeeping and honey production
  • Fish farming where water allows
  • Enterprise selection and market testing

Examples

RabbitsBeekeepingFish farmingValue addition
The wider picture

Every enterprise is part of a system

A healthy dairy herd depends on fodder, fodder depends on soil and water, and income depends on reaching the right buyer. By supporting the whole farm we help farmers see these links and manage them.
