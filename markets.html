Export readiness

What buyers expect

We aim to help farmers and producer groups work toward the standards that open doors.

Quality grading

Consistent size, colour and condition that meet buyer specifications.

Traceability and records

Field-to-shipment records that satisfy audits and buyer questions.

Food safety standards

Hygiene, safe input use and certification such as GlobalG.A.P. where required.

Packaging and cold chain

Packing and temperature control that protect quality in transit.

Phytosanitary compliance

Meeting plant-health rules set by Kenyan and importing authorities.

Reliable buyer relationships

Contracts, communication and delivery performance that build trust.
