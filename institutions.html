Research centres, development agencies, financiers and universities across Africa and the world are driving the science and investment behind food security.

How we engage

Turning institutional knowledge into farm results

We aim to be a practical bridge between research and the farmers who need it.

Learn

Draw on published research, field guides and training from leading institutions.

Test

Pilot proven methods and varieties on partner farms and measure results locally.

Train

Connect farmers and trainers with extension and capacity-building programmes.

Collaborate

Explore joint projects, funding and exchange with institutions in Africa and beyond.
