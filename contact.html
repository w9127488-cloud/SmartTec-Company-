Frequently asked questions

Who can partner with Smart Tec Agricultural Company?

Farmers, cooperatives, agripreneurs, researchers, buyers, technology providers, development agencies and institutions are all welcome to get in touch.

Do you work outside Kenya?

We are based in Kenya and serve farming communities there, with regional and global ambitions. We are open to collaboration across Africa and beyond.

How do technology pilots work?

We begin with a small, supervised trial on a partner farm, measure the cost and the benefit honestly, and only then recommend wider use.
