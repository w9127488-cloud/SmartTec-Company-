Bridging farming tradition with tomorrow’s technology

Smart Tec Agricultural Company exists to make modern agricultural knowledge practical, accessible and valuable to the farmers we reach.

Who we are

An agricultural company built on people, land and opportunity

We envision an agricultural ecosystem in which proven local knowledge works hand in hand with modern tools. Our purpose is to help farmers and agripreneurs make informed decisions, improve resilience, strengthen value chains and explore new opportunities.

We work across crops, livestock, poultry and emerging farm enterprises because real farms do not separate them. Our services and partnerships are developed in stages, matched to farmer needs, local conditions and available resources.

Our mission

Advance smart agriculture and food security

To bring practical technology, trusted knowledge and market opportunity to farmers and agribusiness, so that farms become more productive, more resilient and more profitable.

Our vision

Agriculture that serves people and the land

An agricultural future in which every farmer we reach can make data-informed decisions, protect natural resources and connect with fair and reliable markets.

Our principles

What guides our work

Six commitments shape every project, partnership and piece of advice.

Farmer first

Every tool and service is judged by whether it helps a real farmer make a better decision.

Climate smart

We favour methods that protect soil, save water and build resilience to a changing climate.

Knowledge sharing

Training, demonstration and open exchange of what works on Kenyan farms.

Integrity

Honest advice, verified information and clear limits on what technology can promise.

Inclusive growth

Opportunities for smallholders, women, youth and producer groups alike.

Open to the world

Willing to learn from other countries and adapt, never copy without context.

Our approach

From observation to better decisions

Every engagement follows the same cycle, so lessons from one season improve the next.

Observe

Capture farm conditions, symptoms, weather and production records.

Interpret

Combine the data with local knowledge and qualified expert advice.

Act

Choose a response that is appropriate, safe and affordable.

Learn

Track the results and improve the next decision on the farm.

Who we serve

Working alongside the whole agricultural community

Progress in agriculture is a shared effort. We collaborate with everyone who grows, raises, researches, buys and builds for the food system.

  • Smallholder and commercial farmers
  • Livestock and poultry keepers
  • Cooperatives and producer groups
  • Agripreneurs and young entrepreneurs
  • Researchers and training institutions
  • Buyers, processors and exporters
